1. The Genesis: Preparing SSASIT for the Amroli College Quiz

Bell Ringer was conceptualized and engineered by Ved Mangukiya, an undergraduate student pursuing his Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) at SSASIT (Shree Swami Atmanand Saraswati Institute of Technology) in Surat, Gujarat.

With Amroli College organizing a prestigious Inter-College Business Quiz Competition and SSASIT participating, Ved recognized that conventional memorization and static PDF notes were inadequate for rapid-fire buzzer rounds. He built Bell Ringer to give participating students a high-speed, interactive arena modeled after premier financial broadsheets and dynamic Kahoot-style quiz engines.

3. Design & Architectural Highlights

  • Financial Broadsheet Aesthetic: Hand-crafted dark navy and gold palette, Fraunces headline typography, and a synchronized 42px/s constant velocity ticker tape.
  • Dual Practice Engine: Supports both Kahoot-style 4-Option MCQ (with smart auto-generated distractors and keyboard 1-4 / A-D triggers) and Flashcard Self-Check.
  • Zero-Dependency Vanilla Architecture: Built using 100% standard web technologies (HTML5, modern CSS3 fluid clamp tokens, vanilla JavaScript). Loads in under 50ms with no external telemetry or backend latency.
  • Local Intelligence: Tracks weak categories locally using localStorage to power the "Performance Radar" and enable focused "Review Missed Questions" drills.
